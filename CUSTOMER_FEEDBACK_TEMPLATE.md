## 🎯 Red Flags to Watch For

**Signs customer might not convert:**
- ☐ Rarely uses the system
- ☐ Lots of complaints, little praise
- ☐ Doesn't see value/"meh" responses
- ☐ Avoids talking about pricing
- ☐ Asks to extend free trial

**If you see red flags:**
1. Have honest conversation
2. Ask what's holding them back
3. Decide: Fix issues or move on

## 💡 Tips for Collecting Good Feedback

1. **Ask open-ended questions**
   - Not: "Do you like it?"
   - Better: "What's your experience been like?"

2. **Listen more than you talk**
   - Let customer explain fully
   - Don't defend or justify
   - Just listen and note

3. **Ask "why" three times**
   - Customer: "It's slow"
   - You: "What feels slow?"
   - Customer: "The response time"
   - You: "Why is that a problem?"
   - Customer: "Customers hang up"
   - **Now you understand the real issue!**

4. **Look for patterns**
   - Same complaint multiple times = priority
   - One-off issues = note but don't obsess

5. **Be honest about limitations**
   - "Yes, that's a known issue"
   - "That's on my roadmap"
   - "That's not possible yet"

6. **Thank them for feedback**
   - "This is super helpful"
   - "I really appreciate you taking time"
   - "This will help make it better"
